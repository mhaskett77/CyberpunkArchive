# Field Guide — Cynosure

Capture protocol for Site D and Site C. Written to feed the Semiotics Archive,
which treats environmental detail as primary text.

Plates land in `ENTRY_PLATES` (landscape, captioned, `◳ PLATES` tab) rather than
`ENTRY_IMAGES` (58px portrait slot, built for faces). Target entries: **409**
Cynosure, **307** Project Cynosure, **308** Militech Cerberus, **309** Erebus &
Canto, **528** Katabasis.

---

## 1. Method

**Shoot every subject twice — HUD on, HUD off.** The archive reads in-game UI as
primary text (see the `user: known` and `7 POINTS AVAILABLE` entries). A clean
plate is for architecture; the HUD plate is for the interface layer. Both are data.

**Do not colour-correct.** The archive already tracks amber-incandescent vs
cyan-UI as a dual light-source grammar, and the alchemical colour sequence
(nigredo → albedo → citrinitas → rubedo, entries 502 / 521). Cynosure is lit
almost entirely in the first stage. If you raise exposure to read signage, keep
the uncorrected frame too — the palette is the evidence.

**Photo Mode is unavailable during scripted pursuit.** Expect to lose it while
the Cerberus is actively hunting. Keep a raw screenshot key bound as fallback and
take the ugly frame — a blurry plate of a thing that exists beats no plate.

**Naming**: `sitec_s2_terminal_alpha_hud.jpg`. Site, sector, subject, variant.

**Handoff**: drop the files anywhere in the repo, or paste them in. I'll downsample
to ~1280px / JPEG q75 (≈60–120 KB, matching the existing portrait budget), base64
them into `ENTRY_PLATES`, and write the captions against what's actually in frame.

## 3. Site D — you are here

Entered via the cave beneath the Kress Street apartments, with Bree Whitney.

- **The approach.** The cave mouth and the transition from natural rock to
  built structure. Where does Dogtown stop and Militech begin? That seam is the
  whole point of Site D.
- **Bree's apartment first.** The safe box under the bamboo plant, the room as a
  journalist's room, anything on the walls. Her flat is characterisation you only
  get once.
- **Signage and branding.** Militech marks, NUSA marks, any pre-Unification
  iconography. Date stamps above all — when was this built, when was it sealed?
- **The security layer.** Cameras, turrets, traps. Is this decommissioned
  infrastructure still live, or re-armed by someone? The distinction matters:
  live security means Militech never actually left.
- **Evan McRay's log terminals.** Photograph the screens with text legible, and
  photograph the terminal *hardware* — its age tells you the site's vintage.
- **Dante Caruso.** Militech insignia, rank markings, loadout.
- **The shard on Bree.** Capture the text. Her arrangement — eddies, a ticket out
  of the NUSA, a new life — is the sentence the whole expansion rhymes with.
- **Anything pointing toward Site C.** Site D was a reloading station *connected*
  to the main facility. Look for the connection: a sealed corridor, a tram, a
  freight line, a map. Whether the two sites are physically joined is an open
  question I could not settle from sources.

## 4. Site C — later

<!-- Spoiler territory: this is the Somewhat Damaged descent. -->

Sequence: elevator → Restricted Area A → Sector 1 → Administration → Sector 2 →
sealed gate → Sector 3 → the Core.

- **The elevator.** Reported to be drawn from *Akira* (1988). Shoot the car, the
  shaft, the depth indicator. Confirm or kill the resemblance yourself.
- **Restricted Area A.** The threshold. Whatever is stencilled at the point of no
  return is the inscription over the gate — photograph it as such.
- **Sector 1 → 2 → 3.** One wide orienting plate per sector, plus the dominant
  light source. This is the colour-progression test.
- **Administration.** Where the human bureaucracy of the project lives. Desks,
  rosters, org charts, personal effects. Look for **Lisa Smith** and the
  **Cassel twins** by name — Aurore and Aymeric were on this expedition, and
  finding their names on a wall here connects Cynosure to *Birds with Broken Wings*.
- **The terminal bank.** Callsigns reported as Alpha, Bravo, Sierra, Victor.
  Confirm, and check whether the NATO set continues beyond those four.
- **Day 55.** The shutdown order. If the "not just poking the bear anymore" text
  exists as a readable shard, that plate belongs in entry 307.
- **The countermeasures.** McRay's logs say the team prepared measures to stop the
  AIs taking the core. What do they physically *look* like? This is the most
  interesting unphotographed thing in the facility.
- **The Cerberus.** Model numbers, unit markings, the scanner eye. Count the legs
  in frame — hexapedal, against the 7-system hypothesis. Look for **dormant
  units**: how many were down there?
- **The Core.** Reported as modelled on a Cold War nuclear gyroscope, and as the
  *Akira* core. Shoot it wide, then shoot the housing detail. Note that the Erebus
  is said to resemble it — put the two in frame together if you can.
- **The maintenance room off the mess hall** (Technical Ability gate). The Erebus
  blueprint. If your build can't open it, photograph the closed door — a threshold
  that refuses you is still an archive object.
- **So Mi in the core.** The composition of that shot — where she is placed, what
  surrounds her, how the room frames her — is the Akira comparison standing or falling.

## 5. Questions the sources could not settle

Carried here so exploration has targets. The Cyberpunk wiki was unreachable during
research, so these rest on secondary guides and are worth your own eyes:

1. Are Site C and Site D physically connected in-game, or only narratively?
2. Full NATO callsign set on the terminals — four, or more?
3. Exact wording of McRay's logs and the day-55 shutdown order.
4. Is there Arasaka iconography anywhere in Cynosure? The project was built to
   counter Soulkiller; a captured Arasaka artifact would be significant.
5. Construction and decommission dates on signage.
6. How many Cerberus units are present, and are any others dormant?
7. Does the facility's lighting run an alchemical progression by sector?
8. Any Voodoo Boys or Pacifica surface trace inside the facility.
